# Sources for the Stories
@lead The history boxes in this book simplify, as stories must, but none of them is invented. These are the sources behind each one, in the order they appear, so that you can read the full versions and check the details for yourself.

## Chapter 1

- **APL.** K. E. Iverson, *A Programming Language*, Wiley, 1962; K. E. Iverson, "Notation as a Tool of Thought", 1979 ACM Turing Award Lecture, *Communications of the ACM* 23(8), 1980.
- **Numeric, Numarray and NumPy; LIGO and the black-hole image.** C. R. Harris et al., "Array programming with NumPy", *Nature* 585, 2020; T. E. Oliphant, *A Guide to NumPy*, Trelgol Publishing, 2006.

## Chapter 2

- **"Matrix".** J. J. Sylvester, "Additions to the articles 'On a new class of theorems' and 'On Pascal's theorem'", *Philosophical Magazine* 37, 1850; A. Cayley, "A Memoir on the Theory of Matrices", *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London* 148, 1858.

## Chapter 3

- **Ariane 5 Flight 501.** J.-L. Lions et al., *ARIANE 5 Flight 501 Failure: Report by the Inquiry Board*, European Space Agency and CNES, 19 July 1996.
- **The Patriot missile at Dhahran.** US General Accounting Office, *Patriot Missile Defense: Software Problem Led to System Failure at Dhahran, Saudi Arabia*, GAO/IMTEC-92-26, February 1992.
- **IEEE 754.** *IEEE Standard for Binary Floating-Point Arithmetic*, ANSI/IEEE Std 754-1985; ACM A. M. Turing Award citation for W. Kahan, 1989.

## Chapter 4

- **Nearest neighbours.** E. Fix and J. L. Hodges, *Discriminatory Analysis. Nonparametric Discrimination: Consistency Properties*, Technical Report 4, USAF School of Aviation Medicine, Randolph Field, Texas, 1951.

## Chapter 5

- **Florence Nightingale.** F. Nightingale, *Notes on Matters Affecting the Health, Efficiency, and Hospital Administration of the British Army*, London, 1858; Royal Statistical Society, membership records.
- **Box plots and fences.** J. W. Tukey, *Exploratory Data Analysis*, Addison-Wesley, 1977.

## Chapter 6

- **The condition number.** A. M. Turing, "Rounding-off errors in matrix processes", *Quarterly Journal of Mechanics and Applied Mathematics* 1(1), 1948.
- **Ceres and least squares.** C. F. Gauss, *Theoria Motus Corporum Coelestium*, 1809; A.-M. Legendre, *Nouvelles méthodes pour la détermination des orbites des comètes*, 1805; for the history, S. M. Stigler, *The History of Statistics*, Harvard University Press, 1986.
- **PCA.** K. Pearson, "On Lines and Planes of Closest Fit to Systems of Points in Space", *Philosophical Magazine* 2(11), 1901; H. Hotelling, "Analysis of a complex of statistical variables into principal components", *Journal of Educational Psychology* 24, 1933.
- **The Netflix Prize.** J. Bennett and S. Lanning, "The Netflix Prize", *Proceedings of KDD Cup and Workshop*, 2007; Y. Koren, R. Bell and C. Volinsky, "Matrix Factorization Techniques for Recommender Systems", *IEEE Computer* 42(8), 2009.

## Chapter 7

- **RAND's random digits.** RAND Corporation, *A Million Random Digits with 100,000 Normal Deviates*, Free Press, 1955.
- **Monte Carlo.** N. Metropolis and S. Ulam, "The Monte Carlo Method", *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 44(247), 1949; N. Metropolis, "The Beginning of the Monte Carlo Method", *Los Alamos Science*, Special Issue, 1987.
- **Buffon's needle.** G.-L. Leclerc, Comte de Buffon, *Essai d'arithmétique morale*, 1777.
- **The bootstrap.** B. Efron, "Bootstrap Methods: Another Look at the Jackknife", *The Annals of Statistics* 7(1), 1979; International Prize in Statistics, 2018.

## Chapter 8

- **Fortran.** J. W. Backus et al., "The FORTRAN automatic coding system", *Proceedings of the Western Joint Computer Conference*, 1957.
- **Grace Hopper's nanosecond.** Smithsonian National Museum of American History, collection of Grace Hopper's "nanoseconds".

## Chapter 9

- **Gradient descent.** A.-L. Cauchy, "Méthode générale pour la résolution des systèmes d'équations simultanées", *Comptes rendus de l'Académie des sciences* 25, 1847.
- **Regression to the mean.** F. Galton, "Regression Towards Mediocrity in Hereditary Stature", *Journal of the Anthropological Institute of Great Britain and Ireland* 15, 1886.
- **k-means.** S. P. Lloyd, "Least squares quantization in PCM", *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 28(2), 1982 (Bell Labs memorandum, 1957); J. MacQueen, "Some methods for classification and analysis of multivariate observations", *Proceedings of the Fifth Berkeley Symposium*, 1967; D. Arthur and S. Vassilvitskii, "k-means++: The Advantages of Careful Seeding", *SODA*, 2007.
